Unit economics

Every number, from the ground up

What a cardholder spends 2 transactions × $37.91 average = $75 / month
Domestic — 90% of that volume $67.50 × 0.5% merchant charge = $0.34
Cross-border — 10% of that volume $7.50 × 1.5%, uncapped = $0.11
Revenue per active card $0.34 + $0.11 = $0.45 / month · $5.40 / year
At 10 million active cards 10M × $5.40 = $4.5M / month · $54M / year

*We capture the whole 0.5%, not a slice of it, because we are the scheme, the issuer and the acquirer. Visa and Mastercard net roughly 0.2% of volume as the network alone.

*Card issuance fees, partner-issuer fees and fraud APIs are revenue on top of this and are not counted here.
